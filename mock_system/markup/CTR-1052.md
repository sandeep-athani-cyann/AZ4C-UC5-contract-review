# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

**Contract ID:** CTR-1052

**This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement** is made on 14 September 2026 between **Veridian Logistics Pvt Ltd**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, with its registered office at Andheri East, Mumbai 400069 ("Veridian"), and **Rajhans Fuel Cards Pvt Ltd**, with its registered office in Delhi ("the Supplier").

**Background.** Veridian wishes to engage the Supplier for evaluating a possible commercial relationship for last-mile delivery in Bengaluru, and the parties wish to record the terms on which they will do so.

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

**1.1** **"Agreement"** means this document and its schedules.

**1.2** **"Confidential Information"** means all non-public information disclosed by one party to the other, in any form.

**1.3** **"Purpose"** means the evaluation described in the Background.

**1.4** **"Services"** means the services described in Schedule 1.

**1.5** **"Working Day"** means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in Mumbai.

## 2. Scope

**2.1** Each party may disclose Confidential Information to the other solely for the Purpose.

**2.2** Each party shall appoint a relationship manager who will meet the other party's manager at least once a month.

## 3. Term

**3.1** This Agreement starts on the date above and continues for 12 months unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.

## 4. Confidentiality

**4.1** Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information confidential, use it only for the purposes of this Agreement, and disclose it only to its employees and advisers who need to know it.

**4.2** These obligations do not apply to information that is public, already known to the recipient, or required to be disclosed by law.

## 5. Termination

**5.1** Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within thirty (30) days of notice.

## 6. Conflicts of Interest

**6.1** The Supplier shall promptly disclose to Veridian any actual or potential conflict of interest that arises in connection with the Services, and shall take reasonable steps to manage it.

## 7. Compliance with Laws

**7.1** Each party shall comply with all laws applicable to it in performing this Agreement, including labour, tax and environmental laws, and shall hold all licences needed for its obligations.

## 8. Personnel

**8.1** The Supplier shall ensure that its personnel are suitably qualified, have passed background checks appropriate to the role, and comply with Veridian's site rules, including its policies on safety, conduct and the prevention of sexual harassment at the workplace. Veridian may require the removal of any individual who breaches those rules.

## 9. Waiver

**9.1** A failure or delay in exercising any right is not a waiver of it. A waiver is effective only if given in writing.

## 10. Assignment

**10.1** Neither party may assign or transfer this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, except that Veridian may assign it to an affiliate or to a successor of its business.

## 11. Entire Agreement

**11.1** This Agreement, with its schedules, is the entire agreement between the parties about its subject matter and supersedes all earlier discussions, proposals and understandings.

## 12. Severability

**12.1** If any provision of this Agreement is found invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force, and the parties shall negotiate in good faith a valid provision with the closest possible effect.

## 13. Notices

**13.1** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing in English and delivered by hand, by registered post or by email to the addresses set out at the start of this Agreement. A notice sent by email is deemed received on the next working day.

## 14. Anti-Bribery

**14.1** Each party shall comply with the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988 and all other applicable anti-bribery laws, and shall not offer or accept any improper payment in connection with this Agreement.

## 15. Business Continuity

**15.1** The Supplier shall maintain a business continuity and disaster recovery plan appropriate to the Services, test it at least once a year, and share a summary of the results with Veridian on request.

## 16. Health and Safety

**16.1** While at Veridian's warehouses, hubs or offices, the Supplier's personnel shall follow all safety instructions, wear the protective equipment required on site, and report any incident or near miss to the site manager the same day.

## 17. Dispute Escalation

**17.1** Before starting any formal proceedings, the parties shall try to resolve a dispute by referring it first to their relationship managers and then, if not resolved within fifteen (15) Working Days, to a director of each party, who shall meet within a further ten (10) Working Days. Nothing in this clause prevents a party from seeking urgent interim relief.

## 18. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

**18.1** This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. The courts at Mumbai shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of or in connection with it.
