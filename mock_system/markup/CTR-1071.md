# Statement of Work

**Contract ID:** CTR-1071

**This Statement of Work** is made on 12 September 2026 between **Veridian Logistics Pvt Ltd**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, with its registered office at Andheri East, Mumbai 400069 ("Veridian"), and **Ekam Software Services Pvt Ltd**, with its registered office in Hyderabad ("the Supplier").

**Background.** Veridian wishes to engage the Supplier for the delivery of security guarding at the Bhiwandi warehouse under the Master Services Agreement between the parties, and the parties wish to record the terms on which they will do so.

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

**1.1** **"Agreement"** means this document and its schedules.

**1.2** **"Confidential Information"** means all non-public information disclosed by one party to the other, in any form.

**1.3** **"Fees"** means the charges payable by Veridian for the Services, as set out in Schedule 1.

**1.4** **"Deliverables"** means everything the Supplier produces for Veridian under this Agreement.

**1.5** **"Working Day"** means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in Mumbai.

## 2. Scope

**2.1** The Supplier shall provide security guarding at the Bhiwandi warehouse to Veridian with reasonable skill and care and in accordance with good industry practice.

**2.2** Each party shall appoint a relationship manager who will meet the other party's manager at least once a month.

## 3. Term

**3.1** This Agreement starts on the date above and continues for 36 months unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.

## 4. Fees and Payment

**4.1** Veridian shall pay each undisputed invoice within sixty (60) days of receiving a valid GST-compliant invoice.

**4.2** All Fees are exclusive of GST, which Veridian shall pay at the applicable rate against a valid tax invoice.

## 5. Confidentiality

**5.1** Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information confidential, use it only for the purposes of this Agreement, and disclose it only to its employees and advisers who need to know it.

**5.2** These obligations do not apply to information that is public, already known to the recipient, or required to be disclosed by law.

## 6. Data Protection

**6.1** The Supplier shall process personal data only on Veridian's documented instructions and in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and shall notify Veridian of any personal data breach without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of it.

## 7. Intellectual Property

**7.1** All Deliverables and all intellectual property rights in them shall vest in Veridian on creation. The Supplier hereby assigns all such rights to Veridian.

## 8. Limitation of Liability

**8.1** The Supplier's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed two (2) times the Fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim, save that no limit shall apply to liability for fraud, wilful misconduct or breach of confidentiality.

## 9. Termination

**9.1** Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within thirty (30) days of notice.

## 10. Relationship of the Parties

**10.1** The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.

## 11. Entire Agreement

**11.1** This Agreement, with its schedules, is the entire agreement between the parties about its subject matter and supersedes all earlier discussions, proposals and understandings.

## 12. Personnel

**12.1** The Supplier shall ensure that its personnel are suitably qualified, have passed background checks appropriate to the role, and comply with Veridian's site rules, including its policies on safety, conduct and the prevention of sexual harassment at the workplace. Veridian may require the removal of any individual who breaches those rules.

## 13. Health and Safety

**13.1** While at Veridian's warehouses, hubs or offices, the Supplier's personnel shall follow all safety instructions, wear the protective equipment required on site, and report any incident or near miss to the site manager the same day.

## 14. Change Control

**14.1** Either party may request a change to the Services. The Supplier shall respond within ten (10) Working Days with the impact on scope, timelines and Fees. No change is binding until both parties sign a written change note. The Supplier shall not refuse a reasonable change requested by Veridian.

## 15. Non-Solicitation

**15.1** During the term and for twelve (12) months afterwards, neither party shall solicit for employment any employee of the other who was involved in the Services, without the other's written consent. General advertisements are not solicitation.

## 16. Waiver

**16.1** A failure or delay in exercising any right is not a waiver of it. A waiver is effective only if given in writing.

## 17. Assignment

**17.1** Neither party may assign or transfer this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, except that Veridian may assign it to an affiliate or to a successor of its business.

## 18. Conflicts of Interest

**18.1** The Supplier shall promptly disclose to Veridian any actual or potential conflict of interest that arises in connection with the Services, and shall take reasonable steps to manage it.

## 19. Business Continuity

## 20. Publicity

## 21. Counterparts

**21.1** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together form one instrument.

## 22. Force Majeure

**22.1** Neither party is liable for delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including flood, fire, epidemic, war, strike not involving its own workforce, or government action. The affected party shall notify the other promptly and use reasonable efforts to resume performance.
