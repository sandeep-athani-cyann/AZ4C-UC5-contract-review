# Master Services Agreement

**Contract ID:** CTR-1015

**This Master Services Agreement** is made on 18 October 2026 between **Veridian Logistics Pvt Ltd**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, with its registered office at Andheri East, Mumbai 400069 ("Veridian"), and **Sahyog Pallet Pooling Pvt Ltd**, with its registered office in Ahmedabad ("the Supplier").

**Background.** Veridian wishes to engage the Supplier for the provision of cold-chain transport between Mumbai and Pune, and the parties wish to record the terms on which they will do so.

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

**1.1** **"Agreement"** means this document and its schedules.

**1.2** **"Confidential Information"** means all non-public information disclosed by one party to the other, in any form.

**1.3** **"Fees"** means the charges payable by Veridian for the Services, as set out in Schedule 1.

**1.4** **"Services"** means the services described in Schedule 1.

**1.5** **"Working Day"** means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in Mumbai.

## 2. Scope

**2.1** The Supplier shall provide cold-chain transport between Mumbai and Pune to Veridian with reasonable skill and care and in accordance with good industry practice.

**2.2** Each party shall appoint a relationship manager who will meet the other party's manager at least once a month.

## 3. Term

**3.1** This Agreement starts on the date above and continues for 24 months unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.

## 4. Fees and Payment

**4.1** Veridian shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days of receiving a valid GST-compliant invoice.

**4.2** All Fees are exclusive of GST, which Veridian shall pay at the applicable rate against a valid tax invoice.

## 5. Confidentiality

**5.1** Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information confidential, use it only for the purposes of this Agreement, and disclose it only to its employees and advisers who need to know it.

**5.2** These obligations do not apply to information that is public, already known to the recipient, or required to be disclosed by law.

**5.3** The obligations in this clause shall survive for three (3) years after the expiry or termination of this Agreement.

## 6. Data Protection

**6.1** The Supplier shall process personal data only on Veridian's documented instructions and in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and shall notify Veridian of any personal data breach without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of it.

## 7. Indemnity

**7.1** The Supplier shall indemnify and hold harmless Veridian against all losses arising from (a) any claim that the Services or Deliverables infringe the intellectual property rights of a third party, and (b) any personal data breach caused by the Supplier or its personnel.

## 8. Subcontracting

**8.1** The Supplier shall not subcontract any part of the Services without Veridian's prior written consent.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

**9.1** The Supplier's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the Fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim, save that no limit shall apply to liability for fraud, wilful misconduct or breach of confidentiality.

## 10. Termination

**10.1** Veridian may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time by giving the Supplier not less than sixty (60) days' written notice.

**10.2** Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within thirty (30) days of notice.

## 11. Business Continuity

**11.1** The Supplier shall maintain a business continuity and disaster recovery plan appropriate to the Services, test it at least once a year, and share a summary of the results with Veridian on request.

## 12. Third-Party Rights

**12.1** Nothing in this Agreement confers any right on a person who is not a party to it.

## 13. Severability

**13.1** If any provision of this Agreement is found invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force, and the parties shall negotiate in good faith a valid provision with the closest possible effect.

## 14. Anti-Bribery

**14.1** Each party shall comply with the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988 and all other applicable anti-bribery laws, and shall not offer or accept any improper payment in connection with this Agreement.

## 15. Entire Agreement

**15.1** This Agreement, with its schedules, is the entire agreement between the parties about its subject matter and supersedes all earlier discussions, proposals and understandings.

## 16. Health and Safety

**16.1** While at Veridian's warehouses, hubs or offices, the Supplier's personnel shall follow all safety instructions, wear the protective equipment required on site, and report any incident or near miss to the site manager the same day.

## 17. Dispute Escalation

**17.1** Before starting any formal proceedings, the parties shall try to resolve a dispute by referring it first to their relationship managers and then, if not resolved within fifteen (15) Working Days, to a director of each party, who shall meet within a further ten (10) Working Days. Nothing in this clause prevents a party from seeking urgent interim relief.

## 18. Counterparts

**18.1** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together form one instrument.

## 19. Records

**19.1** The Supplier shall keep complete and accurate records of the Services and the Fees for at least eight (8) years, as required under the Companies Act, 2013 and tax laws.

## 20. Publicity

**20.1** Neither party shall use the other's name, logo or trademarks in any publicity, case study or client list without the other's prior written consent.

## 21. Change Control

**21.1** Either party may request a change to the Services. The Supplier shall respond within ten (10) Working Days with the impact on scope, timelines and Fees. No change is binding until both parties sign a written change note. The Supplier shall not refuse a reasonable change requested by Veridian.

## 22. Non-Solicitation

**22.1** During the term and for twelve (12) months afterwards, neither party shall solicit for employment any employee of the other who was involved in the Services, without the other's written consent. General advertisements are not solicitation.

## 23. Personnel

**23.1** The Supplier shall ensure that its personnel are suitably qualified, have passed background checks appropriate to the role, and comply with Veridian's site rules, including its policies on safety, conduct and the prevention of sexual harassment at the workplace. Veridian may require the removal of any individual who breaches those rules.

## 24. Relationship of the Parties

**24.1** The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.

## 25. Waiver

**25.1** A failure or delay in exercising any right is not a waiver of it. A waiver is effective only if given in writing.

## 26. Force Majeure

**26.1** Neither party is liable for delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including flood, fire, epidemic, war, strike not involving its own workforce, or government action. The affected party shall notify the other promptly and use reasonable efforts to resume performance. If the event continues for more than sixty (60) days, either party may terminate the affected Services on written notice.

## 27. Notices

**27.1** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing in English and delivered by hand, by registered post or by email to the addresses set out at the start of this Agreement. A notice sent by email is deemed received on the next working day.

## 28. Conflicts of Interest

**28.1** The Supplier shall promptly disclose to Veridian any actual or potential conflict of interest that arises in connection with the Services, and shall take reasonable steps to manage it.

## 29. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

**29.1** This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. The courts at Mumbai shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of or in connection with it.
