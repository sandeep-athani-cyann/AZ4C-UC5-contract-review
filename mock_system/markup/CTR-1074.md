# Statement of Work

**Contract ID:** CTR-1074

**This Statement of Work** is made on 11 October 2026 between **Veridian Logistics Pvt Ltd**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, with its registered office at Andheri East, Mumbai 400069 ("Veridian"), and **Dakshin Warehousing Solutions Pvt Ltd**, with its registered office in Hyderabad ("the Supplier").

**Background.** Veridian wishes to engage the Supplier for the delivery of pallet pooling under the Master Services Agreement between the parties, and the parties wish to record the terms on which they will do so.

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

**1.1** **"Agreement"** means this document and its schedules.

**1.2** **"Confidential Information"** means all non-public information disclosed by one party to the other, in any form.

**1.3** **"Fees"** means the charges payable by Veridian for the Services, as set out in Schedule 1.

**1.4** **"Deliverables"** means everything the Supplier produces for Veridian under this Agreement.

**1.5** **"Working Day"** means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in Mumbai.

## 2. Scope

**2.1** The Supplier shall provide pallet pooling to Veridian with reasonable skill and care and in accordance with good industry practice.

**2.2** Each party shall appoint a relationship manager who will meet the other party's manager at least once a month.

## 3. Term

**3.1** This Agreement starts on the date above and continues for 12 months unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.

## 4. Fees and Payment

**4.1** Veridian shall pay each undisputed invoice within sixty (60) days of receiving a valid GST-compliant invoice.

**4.2** All Fees are exclusive of GST, which Veridian shall pay at the applicable rate against a valid tax invoice.

## 5. Confidentiality

**5.1** Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information confidential, use it only for the purposes of this Agreement, and disclose it only to its employees and advisers who need to know it.

**5.2** These obligations do not apply to information that is public, already known to the recipient, or required to be disclosed by law.

## 6. Data Protection

**6.1** The Supplier shall process personal data only on Veridian's documented instructions and in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and shall notify Veridian of any personal data breach without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of it.

## 7. Intellectual Property

**7.1** All Deliverables and all intellectual property rights in them shall vest in Veridian on creation. The Supplier hereby assigns all such rights to Veridian.

## 8. Limitation of Liability

**8.1** The Supplier's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed two (2) times the Fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim, save that no limit shall apply to liability for fraud, wilful misconduct or breach of confidentiality.

## 9. Termination

**9.1** Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within thirty (30) days of notice.

## 10. Health and Safety

**10.1** While at Veridian's warehouses, hubs or offices, the Supplier's personnel shall follow all safety instructions, wear the protective equipment required on site, and report any incident or near miss to the site manager the same day.

## 11. Dispute Escalation

**11.1** Before starting any formal proceedings, the parties shall try to resolve a dispute by referring it first to their relationship managers and then, if not resolved within fifteen (15) Working Days, to a director of each party, who shall meet within a further ten (10) Working Days. Nothing in this clause prevents a party from seeking urgent interim relief.

## 12. Compliance with Laws

**12.1** Each party shall comply with all laws applicable to it in performing this Agreement, including labour, tax and environmental laws, and shall hold all licences needed for its obligations.

## 13. Relationship of the Parties

**13.1** The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.

## 14. Severability

**14.1** If any provision of this Agreement is found invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force, and the parties shall negotiate in good faith a valid provision with the closest possible effect.

## 15. Business Continuity

**15.1** The Supplier shall maintain a business continuity and disaster recovery plan appropriate to the Services, test it at least once a year, and share a summary of the results with Veridian on request.

## 16. Waiver

## 23. Force Majeure
