# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

**Contract ID:** CTR-1004

**This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement** is made on 19 October 2026 between **Veridian Logistics Pvt Ltd**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, with its registered office at Andheri East, Mumbai 400069 ("Veridian"), and **Arunodaya Freight Systems Pvt Ltd**, with its registered office in Ahmedabad ("the Supplier").

**Background.** Veridian wishes to engage the Supplier for evaluating a possible commercial relationship for barcode scanner supply and maintenance, and the parties wish to record the terms on which they will do so.

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

**1.1** **"Agreement"** means this document and its schedules.

**1.2** **"Confidential Information"** means all non-public information disclosed by one party to the other, in any form.

**1.3** **"Purpose"** means the evaluation described in the Background.

**1.4** **"Services"** means the services described in Schedule 1.

**1.5** **"Working Day"** means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in Mumbai.

## 2. Scope

**2.1** Each party may disclose Confidential Information to the other solely for the Purpose.

**2.2** Each party shall appoint a relationship manager who will meet the other party's manager at least once a month.

## 3. Term

**3.1** This Agreement starts on the date above and continues for 24 months unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.

## 4. Confidentiality

**4.1** Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information confidential, use it only for the purposes of this Agreement, and disclose it only to its employees and advisers who need to know it.

**4.2** These obligations do not apply to information that is public, already known to the recipient, or required to be disclosed by law.

## 5. Termination

**5.1** Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within thirty (30) days of notice.

## 6. Records

**6.1** The Supplier shall keep complete and accurate records of the Services and the Fees for at least eight (8) years, as required under the Companies Act, 2013 and tax laws.

## 7. Assignment

**7.1** Neither party may assign or transfer this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, except that Veridian may assign it to an affiliate or to a successor of its business.

## 8. Business Continuity

**8.1** The Supplier shall maintain a business continuity and disaster recovery plan appropriate to the Services, test it at least once a year, and share a summary of the results with Veridian on request.

## 9. Third-Party Rights

**9.1** Nothing in this Agreement confers any right on a person who is not a party to it.

## 10. Health and Safety

**10.1** While at Veridian's warehouses, hubs or offices, the Supplier's personnel shall follow all safety instructions, wear the protective equipment required on site, and report any incident or near miss to the site manager the same day.

## 11. Dispute Escalation

**11.1** Before starting any formal proceedings, the parties shall try to resolve a dispute by referring it first to their relationship managers and then, if not resolved within fifteen (15) Working Days, to a director of each party, who shall meet within a further ten (10) Working Days. Nothing in this clause prevents a party from seeking urgent interim relief.

## 12. Non-Solicitation

**12.1** During the term and for twelve (12) months afterwards, neither party shall solicit for employment any employee of the other who was involved in the Services, without the other's written consent. General advertisements are not solicitation.

## 13. Change Control

**13.1** Either party may request a change to the Services. The Supplier shall respond within ten (10) Working Days with the impact on scope, timelines and Fees. No change is binding until both parties sign a written change note. The Supplier shall not refuse a reasonable change requested by Veridian.

## 14. Compliance with Laws

**14.1** Each party shall comply with all laws applicable to it in performing this Agreement, including labour, tax and environmental laws, and shall hold all licences needed for its obligations.

## 15. Anti-Bribery

**15.1** Each party shall comply with the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988 and all other applicable anti-bribery laws, and shall not offer or accept any improper payment in connection with this Agreement.

## 16. Publicity

**16.1** Neither party shall use the other's name, logo or trademarks in any publicity, case study or client list without the other's prior written consent.

## 17. Relationship of the Parties

**17.1** The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.

## 18. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

**18.1** This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. The courts at Mumbai shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of or in connection with it.


**Signed** for and on behalf of Veridian Logistics Pvt Ltd: Authorised Signatory

**Signed** for and on behalf of Arunodaya Freight Systems Pvt Ltd: Authorised Signatory
